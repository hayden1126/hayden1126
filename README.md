## 🚀 What I'm Currently Building

### **ResearcherX** — Co-founder
An AI-native document editor and **logic validation engine** for researchers, built on **GraphRAG, Neo4j, and LLMs**.

Our core bet is that general-purpose chat models are the wrong primitive for serious research writing. We construct **run-dependent "truth graphs"** scoped to specific fields when writing a paper — enabling deterministic verification of claims, citations, and logical consistency within the work itself.

- 🏆 **Top-6 finalist**, Millard Chan '99 Technology Challenge ($25K prize) — the only undergraduate-led team among finalists
- 🥈 **2nd place**, Columbia startup club competition
- 🎯 Heading toward a pilot deployment at Columbia; early investor conversations underway

### **Teaching Assistant — ENGI 1006** (Columbia University)
TA for Columbia's largest intro CS course (~220 students: *Introduction to Computing for Engineers and Applied Scientists*). I run weekly office hours and coach students through **Python, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, SciPy, and scikit-learn**.
